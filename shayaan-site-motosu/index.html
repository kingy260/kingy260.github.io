About

I'm a first-year MS in Robotics student at Carnegie Mellon University, co-advised by Dr. Ji Zhang (Z-Lab) and Dr. Jonathan Francis (Robot Learning Lab, Bosch Center for AI and CMU). I work on contact-rich, whole-body loco-manipulation skills for humanoids. Right now I'm focused on getting them to lift objects of what I like to call non-trivial weight (heavy!!!).

Before CMU, I studied aerospace engineering at Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU), graduating with distinction and second in my program. There I wrote a survey of machine learning for aerial manipulation with Dr. Farrokh Janabi-Sharifi, tracked space debris with on-orbit star trackers with Dr. John Enright, and spent a year on the Canadarm3 program at MDA Space.

Machine learning for aerial manipulation

Research Assistant · Robotics, Mechatronics, and Automation Laboratory (RMAL), TMU, with Dr. Farrokh Janabi-Sharifi · May 2025 – May 2026

Survey paper

Machine Learning in Aerial Manipulation: A Survey

Shayaan Shaikh, Niloufar Amiri, Shayan Sepahvand, Farrokh Janabi-Sharifi

Robotics and Autonomous Systems Under review

No earlier survey focused on learning for aerial manipulation, even as its use grows quickly. I analyzed the perception, control, and motion planning literature to map open challenges: object detection and localization with pretrained detectors fused with depth, system identification of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) and manipulator dynamics, and learning-augmented model predictive control and reinforcement learning.

Space debris tracking with star trackers

Research Assistant · Space Avionics and Instrumentation Laboratory (SAIL), TMU, with Dr. John Enright · May – Sep 2024

Conference presentation

Space Debris Monitoring Using Star Trackers

Shayaan Shaikh, John Enright

CASI ASTRO 2024, Toronto

Over 100 ST-16 star trackers already in orbit can watch for debris for free. I built a lightweight multi-object tracking pipeline in MATLAB (Kalman filters with Hungarian assignment) that separates resident space objects (RSOs) from background stars, reaching about 70% multi-object tracking accuracy (MOTA) on three flight datasets from Rocket Lab and SAIL, plus a confidence score that flags likely RSOs.

Projects

Autonomous Helicopter Drilling System

Team capstone, TMU · Jan – Apr 2025

Modeled an underactuated 3-DoF Quanser helicopter from first principles and controlled it with cascaded PID loops in Simulink and on hardware, holding under 10% overshoot across its full range of motion. A Kalman filter fusing IMU and encoder data smoothed landings, a camera pipeline found drilling targets with color thresholding and blob detection, and drill control ran in C with limit-switch and IR feedback.

TMU CanSat

LeRobot SO-101 Arm

Personal project · May 2026

3D printed and assembled the open-source SO-101 arm and its teleoperation leader arm, then fine-tuned and deployed SmolVLA, a small open-source vision-language-action (VLA) model, with Hugging Face's LeRobot. Also trained a reinforcement learning policy in MuJoCo for lifting objects with the arm.

Automated PCB Component Detection

Course project, TMU · Oct 2024

Fine-tuned a YOLOv11 detector (Ultralytics, PyTorch) to verify component placement on printed circuit boards (PCBs) across 13 component classes, reaching 81.7% mAP@50, alongside an OpenCV pipeline using image masking and edge detection.
